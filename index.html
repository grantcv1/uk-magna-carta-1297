Magna Carta (1297) in Akoma Ntoso

Edward I’s 1297 confirmation of the Great Charter, as it stands today on legislation.gov.uk. Chapters I, IX and XXIX survive, along with the closing text. The rest have been repealed, and the document records which Act repealed each one. It is marked up in Akoma Ntoso, the OASIS standard for legal and legislative documents.

How it was made

The XML was generated by Claude for Grant Vergottini from the legislation.gov.uk data. Claude didn’t write a word of this text. It marked up the official text and its annotations. The photograph shows the charter with its seal, in the copy owned by David M. Rubenstein and shown at the U.S. National Archives.
